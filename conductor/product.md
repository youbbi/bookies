# Initial Concept

A book sharing and lending platform for friends and family where users can:
- Share books they've read and enjoyed
- See what their friends have read and liked
- Request to borrow books from friends
- Participate in a community-style book club where everyone reads independently but can discover and lend great books within their trusted circle

---

# Product Guide: Bookies

## Vision
Bookies is a book sharing and lending platform that brings the joy of borrowing books back to your personal circle. In a world of impersonal marketplaces and algorithmic recommendations, Bookies creates a trusted space where friends and family can share their favorite reads.

## Target Users
**Casual readers** - People who read a few books per year and want a simple, friendly way to share books with people they know. They value convenience over complexity and trust over features.

## Core Goals
Users should be able to:

1. **Discover books** - See what friends have read and loved, getting authentic recommendations from people they trust rather than algorithms or strangers.

2. **Manage lending** - Keep track of who has their books and coordinate returns with gentle reminders, removing the awkwardness from borrowing.

3. **Build a reading profile** - Log books they've read, rate them, and share mini-reviews with friends to help others decide what to read next.

## Value Proposition
**Trust-based sharing** - Unlike public marketplaces or large social networks, Bookies focuses exclusively on sharing within your personal network of friends and family. Lending a book feels safe because you know exactly who has it.

**Simplicity first** - A streamlined, no-fuss experience designed for casual readers. No complex tracking systems, no social noise—just a clean way to share books with people you care about.

## MVP Features

### Personal Bookshelf
- Add books via search or barcode scan
- Mark books as read, currently reading, or want-to-read
- Indicate which books are available to lend

### Friend Connections
- Add friends to your network
- Browse friends' bookshelves
- See what they've read and enjoyed

### Borrow Requests
- Request to borrow a book from a friend
- In-app messaging to coordinate pickup and return
- Track active loans (both lending and borrowing)

## Platform
**Mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA)** - A responsive web application optimized for mobile that can be installed on home screens, providing a native-like experience while maintaining the accessibility and faster development cycle of web technologies.
